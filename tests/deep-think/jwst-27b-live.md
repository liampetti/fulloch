## Summary

JWST launched on 25 December 2021 after weather-related delays and reached its L2 orbit on 24 January 2022. Commissioning concluded on 11 July 2022, preceding the release of initial scientific data on 12 July 2022. [1]

On 27 March 2018, the launch was delayed to at least May 2020 due to propulsion system issues and potential sunshield snagging. [1]

The telescope is designed to last at least five and a half years, with a goal of ten years. [1]

Fuel to maintain the halo orbit is the expected limiting factor for the telescope's operational lifespan. [1]

## JWST Launch Timeline and Early Operations

The James Webb Space Telescope launched on 25 December 2021, a date set after a delay from the 24 December window due to unfavorable weather forecasts. [1]

The 25 December 2021 launch was the final date, following a delay from the 24 December window due to forecasts of unfavorable weather. [1]

JWST completed its commissioning activities and became ready for full scientific operations on 11 July 2022. [1]
